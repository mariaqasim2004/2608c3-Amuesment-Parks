The Genesis of a Landmark

How 45 Acres of Open Horizon Became the Nation's Crown Jewel

Half a decade ago, the leadership of Seth Groups of Companies recognized that our nation deserved an amusement and entertainment resort capable of rivaling the greatest theme parks in Orlando, Tokyo, and Paris.

Rather than taking shortcuts, the development of Merciado took 5 whole years of meticulous engineering, geological surveys, and architectural craft to achieve the exact finished product envisioned by our founders. Today, it spans an awe-inspiring 45-acre land area, serving over 1.4 million visitors annually with an extraordinary 99.98% operational uptime.

Five Years of Craft

The Roadmap to Perfection

Explore the milestones that transformed 45 acres of raw terrain into the nation's premier theme park.

Year 1

Master Vision & Land Reserve

Seth Group secured the 45-acre master reserve. Environmental clearance, geological foundation testing, and master layout architecture designed with European theme park consultants.

Foundation Laid
Year 2

Subterranean & Water Grid

Construction of 3.2 kilometers of service tunnels, 2-megawatt solar farm, and Neptune Bay's closed-loop 4-million-liter ultrafiltration water treatment complex.

Eco-Infrastructure
Year 3

Heavy Steel Superstructures

Installation of the Viper Hypercoaster track and the 250-foot Titan Drop Tower using precision magnetic braking imported from Germany and Switzerland.

Thrill Coasters Built
Year 4

Theming & Animatronics

Artisans hand-sculpted the Starlight Castle, biomechanical animatronic dinosaurs for Jurassic Safari, and mounted 500,000 architectural LED modules for night spectacles.

Cinematic Theming
Year 5

TÜV Safety & Grand Launch

Over 18 months of simulated passenger load tests, staff hospitality certifications, and international safety accreditation leading to a triumphant national inauguration.
