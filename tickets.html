Are rides included in the admission ticket price? expand_more

Yes! All admission tickets grant unlimited access to all 45+ mechanical rides, interactive theater performances, and water coaster zones throughout the park grounds. Additional charges apply only for arcade midway games and premium merchandise.

Do children under age 3 need a ticket? expand_more

Infants and toddlers under 36 months of age enter completely free with an accompanying adult ticket holder. Identification verifying age may be requested at entry.

Can I purchase FastPass VIP bands on-site at the park? expand_more

A limited quantity of FastPass wristbands are sold each morning at Guest Services, but they routinely sell out. Pre-purchasing online during Step 3 of the booking wizard guarantees your VIP expedited queue privileges.
