Frequently Asked Questions

Merciado is open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM. On Friday, Saturday, and national holiday weekends, operations extend until 11:30 PM with midnight starlight coaster sessions.
Weekday passes (Mon-Fri) offer a reduced admission rate of $39 per adult. Weekend passes are $54 per adult and include extra parade sessions. The Family 4-Pack bundle covers 2 Adults + 2 Kids for $119 on weekdays and $159 on weekends, saving you up to $27.
Under our Seth Group Rain Guarantee Policy, if severe inclement weather forces outdoor coaster operations to suspend for more than 90 consecutive minutes, all guests receive a complimentary Return Pass valid for 12 months.
